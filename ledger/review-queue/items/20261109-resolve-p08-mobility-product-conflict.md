# Resolve P08's mobility claim against the shipped session structure

## Proposal

The frozen P08 Hook and format say mobility can make up half of a session and that mobility blocks are co-equal with strength; positioning pillar 5 and the brand guidelines likewise say mobility is co-primary. The current Product record says sessions are strength-led and mobility appears only in warm-up and cooldown bookends, with no mobility training block. Decide which side changes: change the Product to support the package's co-primary and half-session claims, or revise the brand guideline, positioning pillar, and P08's hypothesis and format to describe the supported bookend role. This proposal changes no frozen Hook. If the Founder chooses to change that Hook, it must enter in a later proposal as a new Angle and needs a kept quote record as sourced pain.

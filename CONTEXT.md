# Context

Glossary for gr0wthlab, the Marketing Engineer's repository.

## Glossary

### Marketing Engineer

A standalone agent, with its own repository, that turns what customers say into marketing for the Founder's Products.
It acts publicly only with the Founder's approval.
_Avoid_: using it to mean a human hire or the Founder.

### Founder

The person who owns the Products and holds every approval the Marketing Engineer needs, including anything published, sent, or paid for.

### Product

An app the Marketing Engineer markets, such as RepToday and later Immerse.
Each Product is built in its own separate repository.

### Dispatch

The Marketing Engineer handing a change to a Product over to firstmate, which carries it out through that Product's normal reviewed path.
The Marketing Engineer reads Products but never changes them itself.

### Go-To-Market Package

A Product's approved marketing decisions, such as its positioning, Fit, approved claims, banned language, and Angles.
Mandu keeps it, and it changes only when the Founder approves a proposed change.
_Avoid_: brain, growth OS, playbook.

### Evidence

What the Marketing Engineer has gathered or observed, such as customer quotes, post results, and the reasons behind rejected Drafts, each with its source.
Evidence can support a proposed change to the Go-To-Market Package but never changes it by itself.

### Draft

A post or message the Marketing Engineer has prepared that no one outside can see.
The Marketing Engineer only ever produces Drafts; every public act, whether posting, sending, or confirming a publishing schedule, is the Founder's.

### Weekly Read

The once-a-week judgment of every live Angle against the signal registered for it before posting, presented to the Founder as keep, kill, change, or test decisions.
Results are never judged between Weekly Reads.

### Instructions

The Marketing Engineer's own working guidance and examples, which it may refine from the Founder's rejection reasons.
Every refinement is shown to the Founder at the next weekly review.

### Guardrail

A limit enforced by the tools the Marketing Engineer works through rather than by its Instructions, such as being able to create only Drafts.
The Marketing Engineer cannot change its own Guardrails.

### Mandu

The Founder's personal agent and repository, which keeps each Product's Go-To-Market Package and applies the changes to it that the Founder approves.
It reads the Marketing Engineer's work to show the Founder status, and never decides for the Founder or records a decision.
_Avoid_: saying the Founder decides in Mandu, or through it.

### Review Queue

The items the Marketing Engineer has prepared that await the Founder's decision.
The Founder gives each decision at a weekly sitting, on a page the Marketing Engineer's repository serves on the Founder's Mac, and nothing but the Founder's own act on that page is ever a decision.
Each item is approved, or rejected with a reason that the Marketing Engineer keeps so later work improves.

### Waitlist

The email sign-up on a Product's website for people who want it before public release.
Each sign-up carries its Source, its answer to the Product's Fit question, and whether the person wants a Beta Build invitation.
The Marketing Engineer works with sign-ups without ever seeing their email addresses; addresses are filled in only when the Founder sends.

### Fit

How closely a person matches the customer a Product is built for.
For RepToday, the best Fit is a busy adult who used to exercise and lost the routine, not a fitness enthusiast.

### Source

The channel a person came from, such as TikTok, YouTube, Reddit, or the Founder's personal network.
A Source names a channel, not an individual post.

### Listening

Gathering Evidence from what people say publicly about the problem a Product solves, such as forum threads and app reviews, without posting or replying.

### Angle

One distinct reason to care about a Product, grounded in a pain real people have stated in their own words, with a citation to where they said it.
An Angle is judged on its own signals, separately from every other Angle.
_Avoid_: concept, theme.

### Hook

The opening words of a post or message that carry an Angle.
One Angle can be tested with several Hooks; a Hook belongs to exactly one Angle.

### Beta Build

The first version of a Product that people outside the Founder's team can install before public release, after Apple has reviewed it for testing.
Recruiting a Beta Cohort waits for it.

### Beta Cohort

The 20 to 50 recruited people who use a Product before public release, whose first sessions are the Product's first real customer evidence.
_Avoid_: tester group, TestFlight cohort.
